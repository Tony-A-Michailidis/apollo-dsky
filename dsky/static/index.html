Cue card: powered descent
  1. V37E63E braking phase. FL V06N61: time to go, time from ignition, crossrange. PRO.
  2. At TIG−35 s the display blanks (average-G on). At TIG−30 s FL V99N62: engine ignition enable. PRO before TIG.
  3. Ignition at 10%, throttle-up at TIG+26 s. Program shows V06N62. Key V16N68E to monitor range, time to go and LR delta-H.
  4. Below 40,000 ft the landing radar locks. Check N68 R3 (LR minus computed altitude), then V57E to let the computer use it.
  5. Put the rendezvous radar in AUTO TRACK if you want the Apollo 11 1202 alarms. On an alarm: V05N09E reads the codes, RSET clears the lamp, KEY REL returns the program display.
  6. P64 comes automatically at high gate: N64 shows time to go, LPD angle, altitude rate, altitude. LPD clicks move the site.
  7. Take over with a ROD click (P66). Hold about −3 ft/s below 200 ft. LUNAR CONTACT, then ENGINE STOP.
  8. P68 confirms the landing: FL V06N43 latitude, longitude, altitude. PRO.

Other things to try: V35E lamp test, V16N36E the clock, V37E52E IMU realign, V37E06E standby, V01N01E 00025E peek at TIME1, V25N69E landing site correction, V69E restart, V36E fresh start.
